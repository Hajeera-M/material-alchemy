# Material Alchemy

Build a Luxury Digital Presence for Ace Spaces & Coro Collective

Create a premium, sophisticated, editorial-quality website for Ace Spaces, with Coro Collective presented as its specialist/child brand.

The website should position the company as a serious, high-end architectural surfaces and material solutions company — not as a generic interior company, furniture company, or simple material supplier.

The goal is to make architects, interior designers, builders, developers, hospitality brands, commercial clients, and premium homeowners immediately understand that this is a company capable of turning advanced surface materials into highly refined architectural applications.

Do NOT copy Corian India, DuPont, or any existing competitor website. Do not reproduce their wording, layout, visual identity, navigation, or page structure.

Use the following information as the CONTENT DIRECTION and INDUSTRY KNOWLEDGE for the website. You should independently decide the best visual language, typography, colour palette, interactions, navigation, spacing, animations, imagery treatment, and overall art direction.

The final result should feel luxurious, contemporary, architectural, credible, and globally positioned.

1. BRAND POSITIONING

Present Ace Spaces as the larger company/platform and Coro Collective as its specialist brand focused on premium surface solutions, material innovation, fabrication, and architectural applications.

The website should communicate:

Do not position the company merely as a "supplier".

The customer should feel that Ace Spaces / Coro Collective can take an architectural idea from concept to finished surface.

3. WHAT THE COMPANY OFFERS
